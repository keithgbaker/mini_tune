# Mini Tune

A small browser demonstration of GazeTrainer's audio zone tones: drag a pointer round a map of the
zones a driver checks and hear each zone's own tone: a pitch set by a simple ratio to the road-ahead
tone, placed in stereo to match the zone's direction. Choose 4 zones (road ahead, rear mirror, left and
right mirrors) or 7 (adding left and right shoulders and the instruments).

Open `index.html` in a browser, tap **Sound on**, and drag. Headphones make the stereo placement clear.

Demonstration only. It is not a training or driving aid and must never be used while driving.
