Mini Tune

Each observation zone around a driver has its own tone: a pitch set by a simple ratio to the road-ahead tone, placed left, centre or right in stereo to match where the zone is. Drag the pointer around the map (headphones help) to hear them.

 

Demonstration only, not a training or driving aid. Use it seated, never while driving.
